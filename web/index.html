FlareOS, running in your browser

Works on iPhone, iPad and desktop. This is the FlareOS kernel from the repo, running inside an x86 emulator (v86). Nothing to install.

Loading the emulator...

On a phone or tablet: drag one finger to move the pointer, tap to click, tap with two fingers for right-click. Text resizes to fit your screen width.

Press Ctrl+Alt+D to switch between the desktop and text mode. On the desktop, the Terminal window runs commands. Try: help, time, uptime, in 60, echo hello, mouse 15 10, beep 440 200.
